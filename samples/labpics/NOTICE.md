# Notice: samples/labpics

The files in this folder are a small extract of **LabPics (Medical) vessel and material segmentation**,
converted to the assayfold record format. They are **not** covered by the MIT
license of this repository's code. They keep the source license: **MIT**.

- Source: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4736111
- License: MIT (https://opensource.org/license/mit)
- License checked at: https://zenodo.org/api/records/4736111 on 2026-11-18
- License statement as found: Zenodo record metadata: license id "mit-license" (record "LabPics dataset for visual understanding of Medical and Chemistry Labs")
- Attribution: Eppel, S., Xu, H., Bismuth, M., Aspuru-Guzik, A. Computer Vision for Recognition of Materials and Vessels in Chemistry Lab Settings and the Vector-LabPics Data Set. ACS Central Science 6, 1743-1752 (2020). doi:10.1021/acscentsci.0c00460 ; data: Eppel, S., Xu, H., Aspuru-Guzik, A., Bismuth, M. (2021) doi:10.5281/zenodo.4736111

Changes made: a subset of records was selected; fields were mapped to the assayfold
schema; unit strings were normalized (the original string is kept in `raw_unit_str`);
provenance and a record hash were added. See `meta.json` for how the sample was built.
